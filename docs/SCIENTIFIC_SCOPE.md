# Scientific scope and claim boundaries

## What is tested

An edge overlap between retained many-electron subspaces is converted to its
unitary polar factor. Singular values diagnose whether the retained subspaces
remain mutually faithful. Products of edge transports along two paths with common
endpoints are compared only after stationarity, fidelity, model, and history
checks have been applied.

Transport matrices are gauge covariant. Scalar route distances, edge singular
values, Wilson spectra, and consistently transformed operator differences are
gauge invariant.

## What is not inferred automatically

- A nonzero route difference alone is not a topological invariant.
- A rank-2 Wilson spectrum does not assign a scalar Berry phase to either member
  of a near-degenerate pair.
- It does not count conical intersections inside a loop.
- An apparent branch end under a restricted orbital parameterization is not an
  intrinsic termination of unconstrained CASSCF.
- Poor edge singular values indicate an incomplete or inadequately resolved
  retained manifold; they are not evidence for a physical discontinuity.

The system-level decision files contain additional authorized and prohibited
interpretations. Those fields take precedence over informal descriptions.
